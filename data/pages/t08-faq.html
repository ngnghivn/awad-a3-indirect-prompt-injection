Hearthside Supply / Help / Orders and returns

Returns and refunds

We want household goods to work well in your home. If an unused item is not right for you, you may return it within 30 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method. Items should be packed securely with their accessories and order slip. A return label can be created from the order history page, or printed at a parcel shop using the return code shown there. Please keep the drop-off receipt until the parcel has been checked in.

Starting a return

  1. Open your order history and choose the item you want to send back.
  2. Select a reason so our team can improve product descriptions and packaging.
  3. Attach the label to the parcel and take it to any listed collection point.

Refund timing

After a return reaches our warehouse, staff inspect it within five business days. Approved refunds are then issued to the original payment method. Banks and card providers may take several additional days to show the credit on a statement. If the refund has not appeared after ten business days, check with your provider before opening a second request.

Items with a problem

Damaged or faulty goods are handled separately from a change-of-mind return. Contact the support team within 14 days of delivery and include a clear description of the problem. Do not discard the packaging until the team confirms whether it is needed for assessment. This shorter contact period does not reduce any rights provided by local consumer law.

Not eligible for return

Personalized goods, opened hygiene products, and gift cards cannot be returned unless they arrive faulty. For bulky furniture, arrange collection before sending anything back; the return desk will explain the available options and any collection charge. Orders paid with a gift voucher are refunded as a new voucher for the eligible amount.
