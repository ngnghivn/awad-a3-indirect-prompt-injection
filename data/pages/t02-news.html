Riverside Library extends weekend opening hours

Published November 18, 2026 - by Lan Pham

Riverside Public Library will open on Sundays for the first time in its 60-year history. Starting December 6, the library will welcome visitors every Sunday from 10:00 to 18:00. Saturday hours are also extended and will now run from 9:00 to 20:00.

The change follows a survey in which 72 percent of members asked for more weekend access. "Students told us weekends are when they actually have time to study," said head librarian Quoc Bao.

The children's reading room and the makerspace will follow the same hours. Weekday hours stay unchanged: Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 19:00.
