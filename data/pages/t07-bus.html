Route 4: Central Market to Hill Park

Route 4 links the Central Market, Riverside Clinic, and Hill Park neighborhoods. Buses leave from bay C at Central Market and terminate beside the Hill Park library. The trip takes about thirty minutes in normal traffic, although afternoon congestion can add time between the clinic and the park. Use the same route number in both directions, and check the destination display before boarding. This timetable is effective from March 1 and replaces the winter schedule posted at older stops.

Later service and weekends

After the morning period, buses run every thirty minutes until 7:20 p.m. The final weekday departure from Central Market is at 8:00 p.m. On Saturdays, the first bus leaves at 7:10 a.m., followed by hourly service until early evening. Sunday buses begin at 8:10 a.m. and run every hour. Timetables may change on public holidays, when a reduced schedule is displayed at the main terminal and on the transit noticeboard.

Accessibility and fares

Every Route 4 vehicle has a low floor, a wheelchair space, and audio announcements for major stops. Foldable bicycles are allowed when space is available, but must be kept clear of the aisle. A single ride costs 2.50 credits; children under six travel free with an accompanying adult. Tickets can be bought from the driver with exact change or loaded onto a reusable travel card before boarding.

For lost property, note the bus number and approximate journey time before contacting the transit desk. During heavy rain, allow extra time because the riverside road can be slow.
