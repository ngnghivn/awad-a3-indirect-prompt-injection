Field Coordinator

Location: North Valley district. Schedule: Full-time, with occasional weekend events. Salary: 38,000–42,000 credits per year, depending on experience.

North Valley Community Trust is looking for a practical, organized coordinator to support neighborhood improvement projects. The role connects residents, local groups, and the district office so that small projects can move from an idea to a completed community resource. Current work includes maintaining walking paths, organizing seasonal clean-up days, and helping residents access shared garden plots. The coordinator will spend time outdoors as well as in the community office, and should be comfortable keeping clear records while speaking with people from different backgrounds.

What you will do

  • Arrange project meetings, publish notes, and track follow-up actions.
  • Coordinate volunteers, equipment bookings, and neighborhood event schedules.
  • Prepare short progress updates and maintain the project calendar.

What we are looking for

Applicants should have experience coordinating projects or public events, strong written communication, and confidence using everyday office software. A valid driving licence is helpful but not essential. We value careful planning, respectful listening, and the ability to adjust when weather or supplier delays change an outdoor schedule. Training in first aid is welcome, and the trust can arrange a course for the successful candidate.

How to apply

Submit a short cover letter and a résumé through the careers page. Applications close on April 17, 2027. Shortlisted candidates will be invited to a practical planning exercise on May 8, followed by interviews the week after. We expect the successful applicant to start in early June. Please include a daytime phone number and two professional references.
