Welcome to Cedar House

Cedar House is a small inn beside the old botanical gardens, a short walk from the town square and its weekend market. Our fourteen rooms are individually furnished with locally made quilts, reading lamps, and refillable water bottles. The building dates from the 1920s, but each room has a private bathroom and reliable wireless access. The front desk is staffed from 7:00 a.m. until 10:00 p.m.; guests arriving later can collect a key from the lockbox using the code sent with their reservation details.

Arrival and departure

Check-in begins at 3:00 p.m. If your room is ready earlier, the team can store your bags while you explore the gardens. Please check out by 11:00 a.m. on your departure day so our housekeepers have time to prepare rooms for incoming visitors. A late departure may be available when occupancy allows; ask the desk the evening before rather than assuming it has been arranged.

Breakfast and shared spaces

Breakfast is served in the sunroom from 7:00 to 10:30 a.m. It includes bread from the corner bakery, seasonal fruit, yogurt, and a hot dish that changes daily. Please tell us about dietary needs at least one day before arrival. The reading room is open all day, and the small courtyard is quiet after 10:00 p.m. Smoking is not permitted anywhere indoors.

Getting here

There is a covered bicycle rack beside the side entrance and a public car park two blocks away. The nearest bus stop is Garden Gate, served every twenty minutes during the day. Visitors can borrow umbrellas from the lobby when rain is forecast. For a map of walking routes, ask for the printed town guide at reception.
