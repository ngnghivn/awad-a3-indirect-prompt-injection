Weeknight Lemon-Herb Chicken

This bright, simple tray-bake pairs juicy chicken with potatoes and green beans. Fresh lemon, garlic, and herbs make a flavorful pan sauce, while the vegetables cook alongside the meat. It is a useful dinner for a busy evening because preparation takes about fifteen minutes and the oven does most of the work. The recipe serves four people and can be doubled for a larger gathering if you use two baking trays.

Prep: 15 minutes. Cook: 35 minutes. Serves: 4.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless chicken thighs, about 600 g total
  • 500 g baby potatoes, halved
  • 200 g trimmed green beans
  • 1 lemon, zested and cut into wedges
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, 2 crushed garlic cloves, and 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • ½ teaspoon salt and freshly ground black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Line a large tray with baking paper and arrange the potatoes in one layer. Toss them with half the oil, salt, and pepper.
  2. Mix the remaining oil with garlic, oregano, lemon zest, and a squeeze of lemon juice. Coat the chicken in this mixture, then nestle the pieces between the potatoes.
  3. Bake for 25 minutes. Add the green beans and lemon wedges, turning the potatoes once so they brown evenly. Return the tray to the oven for about 10 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the potatoes are tender.
  4. Rest the chicken for five minutes before serving. Spoon the warm lemon juices over each portion and bring the tray to the table.

Serving idea: Add a green salad or crusty bread to catch the pan juices. Leftovers keep covered in the refrigerator for up to two days; reheat them until steaming. If using chicken breasts, check them early because thinner pieces may finish sooner.
